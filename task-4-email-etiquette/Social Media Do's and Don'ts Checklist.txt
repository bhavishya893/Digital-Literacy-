Social Media Do's and Don'ts Checklist 

 Do’s

* Think before posting:

When you are about to post any information or media, think before you do so. Ask yourself whether the information or media you are posting are appropriate and whether you would be comfortable if your teachers, family, or employers saw them. Once you have posted your information or media, it may not be easy to delete it.

* Maintain a professional profile:

Your social media profile reflects your personality. Try to maintain a decent profile picture and a simple profile description. Ensure that your profile does not contain any information that may damage your image. A professional profile may help you stand out.

* Use privacy settings:

Social media websites give you the option to set your privacy settings. Ensure that your social media profiles are not accessed by anyone and everyone. This may help you avoid any misuse of your information.

* Verify information before sharing:

Not all information that you read on the internet may be true. Sharing false information may create confusion and may even lead to rumors. Verify the information before sharing it.

* Engage positively:

Social media is used for connecting, learning, and sharing ideas. Always be respectful in your comments or messages, be supportive, and avoid unnecessary arguments. This will definitely help you build a good reputation on social media.

**Don’ts**

* Don’t share sensitive information:

Sharing your personal contact number, home address, passwords, or bank account numbers on social media is not recommended. Hackers or scammers might misuse your personal information, which could be risky for your account security.

* Don’t post inappropriate or offensive content:

Content that is abusive, vulgar, or disrespectful in any manner could affect your reputation in the future. Always ensure that your posts reflect maturity and responsibility.

* Don’t engage in cyberbullying:

Making fun of other people or spreading rumors about them could have a serious impact on their mental health. Always be kind and respectful towards others. Avoid any form of cyberbullying or harassment.

* Don’t trust unknown accounts blindly:

Accepting friend requests or clicking on any links from unknown people could put your account in danger. Always verify the identity of people before getting connected with them on social media.

* Don’t overuse social media: 

Using too much time on social media could affect your studies or productivity. It could also have an impact on your mental health. Always be cautious of using social media in a balanced manner.

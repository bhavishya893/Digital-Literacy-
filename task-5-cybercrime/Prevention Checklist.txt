Prevention Checklist:

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank details with anyone, even if they claim to be from the bank.
Always verify links and messages before clicking, especially from unknown numbers or emails.
Do not accept or approve unknown payment requests or “collect requests” on UPI apps.
Avoid scanning QR codes from unknown sources, as they may lead to money deduction instead of receiving money.
Use only trusted and official apps/websites for transactions and online activities.
Enable app locks and two-factor authentication (2FA) for added security.
Be cautious of offers that seem too good to be true, such as easy money or fake job opportunities.
Regularly check your bank statements and transaction history for any suspicious activity.
Immediately report any fraud on the National Cyber Crime Portal (cybercrime.gov.in).
Call the Cyber Crime Helpline – 1930 as soon as you notice any unauthorized transaction.

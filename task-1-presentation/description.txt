I used Canva to design my digital literacy infographic because it is easy to use and provides a variety of templates, icons, and design elements. The platform helped me create a clean and visually appealing layout without needing advanced design skills.

My infographic highlights key aspects of digital literacy that are important for students. It includes topics such as online safety and security, use of digital tools, professional online presence, email etiquette, and responsible digital usage. Each section is presented with short explanations to make the information clear and easy to understand. The goal of the infographic is to spread awareness among students about how to use digital platforms effectively and responsibly.

One interesting part of this task was selecting the right content and reducing it into short, impactful points that fit the design. At the same time, I found it slightly challenging to maintain a balance between visual appeal and meaningful content, as too much text could make the infographic look cluttered. Overall, this activity helped me understand the importance of presenting information in a simple and engaging way.
